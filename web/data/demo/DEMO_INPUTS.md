# Demo Inputs

For the EcoSort AI local prototype, the Vision Classification layer relies on filename heuristics to ensure 100% reliable demonstrations during your final presentation.

Please prepare or download 6 generic sample images and name them exactly as follows before your demo:

1. `banana.jpg` (An image of a banana peel)
2. `bottle.jpg` (An image of a plastic water bottle)
3. `glass.jpg` (An image of a glass bottle or jar)
4. `mobile.jpg` (An image of an old mobile phone)
5. `battery.jpg` (An image of an AA or lithium battery)
6. `unknown.jpg` (An image of something ambiguous, like a crumpled piece of foil)

**Why?**
The mock classifier looks for these exact words in the filename to guarantee it retrieves the correct RAG rules live. Do NOT use actual copyrighted images in your repository; just save standard fair-use or public domain images locally to your desktop before your 1M1B presentation.
