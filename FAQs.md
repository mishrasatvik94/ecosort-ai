**1M1B \- AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship**

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**

### **1\. Is there any fee to join the internship?**

No. The internship is **completely free of cost**. There are **no registration fees** or hidden charges.

### **2\. Will I receive a stipend for this internship?**

No, this is an **unpaid internship**. However, you will receive valuable learning, project experience, digital credentials, and an official certificate upon successful completion.

### **3\. How will communication happen during the internship?**

All communication will happen **only through your registered email ID and the [Important Internship Communication](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TuSWjdU5ZsGnsaPxD4b89hEuRouXCOZkrp-8oMV2cW4/edit?tab=t.0) document**.  
 Please check this [shared folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1WCR3LtMdaCqQPKTdUvEpTDCk0k7weR-T?usp=drive_link) regularly for updates.

### **4\. Is there a WhatsApp or Telegram group for this internship?**

Currently, **no WhatsApp/Telegram group** is being created. All important information will be shared via email.

### **5\. Where will I get the session schedule and meeting links?**

The session schedule and the meeting link are updated [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1g_XPsWLFE9QBruTfC_jQyhDhjt8S5KxfDpV7b7_FjD4/edit?usp=drive_link).  
 Please keep checking this document regularly for any changes in schedule.

### **6\. Will session recordings be available?**

Yes. **Recordings of the sessions will be available** [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1f5bBvkMm0kzYBvXPpToH1UUpn9KMWv8U?usp=drive_link) after each session.  
 

### **7\. What is the first assignment or task?**

Your **first assignment** is to complete the **Guided Learning Experience** on IBM SkillsBuild using the course link [here](https://skills.yourlearning.ibm.com/activity/PLAN-56138075980A?ngo-id=0331&utm_campaign=aca-1M1B-Internship-Aug26). It is also shared in the email.  
 This is mandatory and forms part of your internship requirements.

### **8\. How will the remaining assignments or tasks be shared?**

Further tasks, project work, and weekly assignments will be **shared during the internship sessions** and through email.

### **9\. Will I get a certificate?**

Yes. Students who complete all requirements including assignments, learning modules, and final project will receive a **co-certified digital certificate** from **IBM SkillsBuild** and **1M1B Foundation**.

### **10\. Do I need a laptop?**

Yes. You will need a **laptop or desktop** with good internet connectivity to complete sessions and assignments.

### **11\. Is attendance compulsory?**

Participation is expected for all key sessions. If you miss a live session, you can watch the recording.  
 However, **assignments must be completed** to receive the certificate.

### **12\. What kind of project do I need to work on?**

You can choose **any project topic related to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**.  
 Your project should connect AI with sustainability, climate action, or any SDG-aligned challenge, using the IBM technologies shared as part of the guided learning program.

Complete guidelines for the project creation will be shared during the internship. 

### **13\. Can I do the project in a team?**

No. You need to complete the project **individually (solo)** 

**14\. How to complete the Mind Meets Machine task?**

Click on the task, go through the content, click on the Mark **Complete** button on the right side.

### **15\. How to complete the lab as part of Guided Learning Experience (GLE)?**

Click on any lab you wish to learn about. Follow the steps to learn further. When you reach the end of the lab, you can choose between the options of working remotely on google collab or working locally on your computer.

Exit the Module. That marks the completion of the labs. 

**17\. Where can I submit my project?**

We will share the details during the internship 

**18\. Can I submit just the workflow if the prototype is not completely developed?**

Yes, you can submit the workflow, idea, architecture of the project, even if the prototype is not completely developed. We would like to focus on your ideas and how you intend to use the IBM technologies and the learnings from the sessions.

**19\. What is the duration of the internship?**  
 The internship duration will be shared in your onboarding email. It typically spans six weeks including learning sessions and project work.

**20\. What are the expected learning outcomes from this internship?**  
 You will gain foundational knowledge in Artificial Intelligence, understand sustainability challenges, and learn how to build AI-driven solutions aligned with real-world problems.

**21\. Do I need prior knowledge of AI or coding?**  
 No prior experience is required. The internship is designed for beginners as well as learners with basic knowledge.

**22\. Which platform will be used for learning?**  
 You will primarily use IBM SkillsBuild along with guided sessions and resources shared by the team.

**23\. What support will I receive during the internship?**  
 You will receive guidance through sessions, email support, and structured learning resources to help you complete tasks and projects.

**24\. Can I use this internship experience in my resume?**  
 Yes. You can mention this internship along with your project work, skills learned, and certifications earned.

**25\. Will there be live sessions or only self-paced learning?**  
 The internship includes a combination of live expert sessions and self-paced learning modules.

**26\. What kind of AI tools or technologies will be covered?**  
 You will explore beginner-friendly AI concepts and tools, including those available through IBM SkillsBuild, focused on solving sustainability challenges.

**27\. What if I face technical issues during the internship?**  
 You can report issues through the official communication channel (email), and the support team will assist you.

**28\. How much time should I dedicate weekly?**  
 You should plan to spend a few hours each week attending sessions and completing assignments.

**29\. What makes this internship different from others?**  
 This internship uniquely combines AI learning with sustainability impact, allowing you to work on real-world problem statements aligned with global goals.

**30\. Can I continue working on my project after the internship ends?**  
 Yes. You are encouraged to further develop and scale your project beyond the internship.
